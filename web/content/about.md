HuddleScope is a non-commercial, independent news aggregator for NFL-related content. Our goal is to provide a clean, modular platform for ingesting news feeds, storing stories, and serving static pages.

## Purpose

This project demonstrates a modular, adapter-based architecture where the Go engine remains independent of hosting, database, and rendering.

## Privacy

We do not use external tracking scripts or analytics. Your browsing activity is not monitored or collected.

## Legal

- This project does not commit full copyrighted articles.
- No unlicensed images are stored or distributed.
- All data comes from publicly available RSS feeds; users are responsible for complying with feed source terms of service.

See [Editorial/Source Policy](https://github.com/HuddleScope/huddlescope.github.io/blob/main/docs/editorial-policy.md) for our detailed editorial boundaries, copyright approach, and operational practices, including:

- What we publish (sources, publisher credits, original links, short excerpts only)
- RSS availability is not blanket permission (per-source `license` and `acceptable_use`)
- Rate limiting and etiquette
- Data deletion and opt-out requests

### Feed disable and removal

If a source publisher requests removal of their feed or specific content, see [Ops Runbook](https://github.com/HuddleScope/huddlescope.github.io/blob/main/docs/ops-runbook.md#feed-disable-procedure) for the disable procedure and [Data deletion](https://github.com/HuddleScope/huddlescope.github.io/blob/main/docs/editorial-policy.md#data-deletion-and-opt-out-requests) in the editorial policy.
